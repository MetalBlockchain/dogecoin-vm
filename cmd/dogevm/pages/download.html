What it does

  • Both networks, one key. Your key has the same address on Dogecoin and on DogecoinVM, and the app shows both balances side by side.
  • Review before you sign. Every payment is shown line by line (who's paid, change back to you, a withdrawal's destination, the fee) before Touch ID signs it. The app then checks the signed transaction says exactly that before sending it.
  • Move between chains in one step. Move DOGE from your Dogecoin balance to DogecoinVM, and withdraw back to your own Dogecoin address.
  • Your key stays on your Mac, encrypted to its Secure Enclave, so the file is useless on any other computer.
  • Notifications when a deposit is credited or a withdrawal is paid.
  • Automatic updates, installed only if they're signed with the release key.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded DogecoinVM-Wallet.dmg.
  2. Drag DogecoinVM Wallet into Applications.
  3. Open it, then create a wallet or import the key you use on this site.

Prefer the browser? The bridge page is a full wallet too, and imports the same key.

Beta. The app works with real DOGE and the bridge's beta limits. Keep amounts small, and back up your key from the app's Settings.
