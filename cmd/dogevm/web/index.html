Use DOGE on DogecoinVM beta

DogecoinVM is a Dogecoin-compatible chain running on Metal Blockchain. Move DOGE over one for one, pay in seconds, and move it back to Dogecoin.

The peg

Every DOGE on DogecoinVM is backed by DOGE locked on Dogecoin. The peg is fully backed while the locked bar is at least as long as the circulating one.
